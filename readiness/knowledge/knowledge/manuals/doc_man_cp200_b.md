# ApexFlow CP-200 Centrifugal Heavy-Duty Feed Pump Operations & Maintenance Manual

## SEC-CP200-01: Pump Alignment & Dynamic Vibration Limits

ApexFlow CP-200 requires laser coupling alignment within 0.05 mm angular and 0.03 mm radial offset. ISO 10816-3 overall vibration velocity must remain below 4.5 mm/s RMS in zone A/B. Vibration exceeding 7.1 mm/s RMS indicates severe mechanical unbalance or bearing degradation.

## SEC-CP200-02: Hydraulic Performance, Cavitation & NPSH Requirements

Net Positive Suction Head required (NPSHr) is 3.2 meters at best efficiency point. Suction pressure dropping below 1.2 bar gauge results in cavitation bubble collapse against the first-stage impeller eye, characterized by popping/gravel rattling noise and hydraulic shock pulses.

## SEC-CP200-03: Mechanical Seal & API Plan 11 Flush Regulation

Fitted with cartridge mechanical seal. API Plan 11 flush circulation delivers process fluid from discharge orifice back to seal chamber. Orifice blockage starves seal faces of lubrication, causing dry running, face scorch marks, and subsequent fluid leakage from the gland drain.

## SEC-CP200-04: Bearing Housing Maintenance & Active Clearances

Equipped with deep-groove 6312 ball bearings (drive end) and paired angular contact 7312 bearings (non-drive end). Maximum allowable radial play is 0.038 mm (nominal 0.025 mm). Relubricate with synthetic ISO VG 68 polyurea grease every 2,000 operating hours. Overgreasing induces churning and temperatures above 85°C.

## SEC-CP200-05: Impeller Vane Pass Pressure Pulsation & Acoustic Resonance

CP-200 5-vane impellers operating below 65% minimum continuous stable flow (MCSF) generate acoustic pressure pulsation at 5x running speed. If pulsation amplitude exceeds 1.5 bar peak-to-peak at the discharge nozzle, verify minimum flow bypass valve position and examine diffuser ring for erosion.
