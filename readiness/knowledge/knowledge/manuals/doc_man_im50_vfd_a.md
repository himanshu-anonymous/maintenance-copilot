## SEC-VFD-01: PWM Carrier Frequency Harmonics & Shaft Voltage Discharge

CRITICAL APPLICABILITY: This section applies solely to DuraTorq-IM50-VFD equipped with external inverter drives. High PWM carrier frequency (>8 kHz) induces common-mode shaft voltages causing electrical discharge machining (EDM) fluting across bearing raceways. Inspect carbon grounding brush and verify carrier setting.
