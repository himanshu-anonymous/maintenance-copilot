## SEC-CP100-01: General Construction & Belt Drive Overview

ApexFlow CP-100 is an end-suction single-stage utility pump. Unlike CP-200, CP-100 incorporates an external spring-loaded swing check valve directly bolted to the discharge flange.

## SEC-CP100-02: Discharge Check Valve Flutter & Line Pulsation

Discharge pressure pulsations on CP-100 models are predominantly caused by worn hinge pins or broken return springs in the discharge swing check valve, creating high-frequency disc chatter and gauge oscillation. Do not confuse with impeller acoustic resonance.

## SEC-CP100-03: Packing Gland & Component Seal Adjustments

Component mechanical seal with external spring. Allow 10-15 drops per minute leakage during running-in; steady stream signifies cracked silicon carbide primary ring.
