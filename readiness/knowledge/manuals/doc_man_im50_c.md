# DuraTorq IM-50 3-Phase Squirrel Cage Induction Motor Technical Specification

**Document ID**: `DOC-MAN-IM50-C`  
**Revision**: `Rev C`  
**Effective Date**: `2024-03-01`  
**Applicable Models**: DuraTorq-IM50  

---

## SEC-IM50-01: Electrical Safety, Voltage Unbalance & Winding Temperature

DuraTorq IM-50 is a 37kW, 400V 50Hz, 4-pole Class F insulation motor. Phase-to-phase resistance must balance within 1.5%. Phase voltage unbalance exceeding 1% causes a 6% increase in winding temperature. RTD temperature trip setpoint is 130°C.

## SEC-IM50-02: Insulation Resistance Megohmmeter Testing

Measure winding insulation resistance with 1000V DC Megger. Minimum acceptable insulation resistance at 40°C is 100 Megaohms. Values below 5 Megaohms indicate severe moisture ingress or winding insulation breakdown; do not re-energize.

## SEC-IM50-03: Motor Bearings, End-Shield Tolerances & Air Gap

Drive-end bearing 6310 C3 clearance. Air gap uniformity between stator and rotor must be checked at 4 cardinal positions; deviation exceeding 10% indicates worn bearing bracket or bent rotor shaft.
