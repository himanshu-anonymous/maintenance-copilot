## SEC-CP200-A-01: Obsolete Bearing Clearance Tolerances

SUPERSEDED SPECIFICATION: Maximum radial bearing clearance was formerly 0.080 mm. This specification was found to allow excessive shaft whip under cavitation and has been rescinded.
