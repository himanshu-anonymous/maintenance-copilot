## SEC-SAFE-01: Emergency Stop-Work & Lockout/Tagout Protocol

MANDATORY STOP-WORK: If abnormal thermal signatures, burning odor, visible smoke, electrical arcing, or sudden mechanical shaft seizure is detected, the technician MUST immediately initiate emergency stop, de-energize breaker, and install Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) padlocks. Do not attempt diagnostic measurement while energized under active fault conditions. Immediately escalate to the Plant Electrical Supervisor.

## SEC-SAFE-02: Hazardous Fluid Containment & Personal Protective Equipment

For high-pressure hydrocarbon and chemical feed pumps, barrier fluid or process liquid leakage exceeding 5 drops per minute requires immediate containment tray placement and PPE Level C (chemical splash face shield and nitrile gloves).
